OUR WORKFLOW

From first input to finished piece.

Each stage moves the product closer to a repeatable, approved result.

01

Brief & interpretation

We review the design or reference and identify the fabric, shape and details the product needs.

02

Fabric & cutting

Material development and cutting establish the physical foundation for the first sample.

03

Sample & print

The design becomes a sample, including print and other visible details where the brief calls for them.

04

Testing & approval

Fabric tests and sample reviews inform refinements. A production sample sets the final reference.

05

Garment making & finish

The approved piece enters production, sewing and finishing with the final sample as guide.
