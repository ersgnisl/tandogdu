THE TWO-WEEK POSSIBILITY

A fast route when the brief allows it.

For suitable work, a two-week turnaround is possible. The final schedule depends on the fabric, design, quantities, approvals and finishing requirements.

Keeping fabric production close to garment making helps us reduce handoffs and make decisions without losing sight of the full product. That creates room to respond quickly when timing is important.

We discuss timing early, then confirm a workable schedule for each project. Sampling and production approvals are key milestones, because speed only works when the right decisions are clear.
