WHO WE ARE

One connected production partner.

A good garment begins long before the sewing stage. It starts with understanding the brief, choosing the right fabric and making deliberate development decisions.

Our work brings fabric, sampling and manufacturing into a shared process. Customers come to us with a design or visual reference; we interpret it, prepare a physical sample and refine it for production.

Producing our own fabrics is a practical advantage. It lets us follow material decisions closely and respond to the needs of a product as it develops. The result is a more considered route from concept to finished garment.
